Welcome to ATHARRYS PROPERTIES LIMITED

Your Trusted Real Estate Partner Since 2009

ATHARRYS PROPERTIES LIMITED was founded with a simple yet powerful vision: to transform the real estate experience in Nigeria by providing exceptional service, transparency, and expertise to every client we serve.

Over the past 15 years, we have grown from a small family-owned business to one of Nigeria's leading real estate companies, helping thousands of families, investors, and businesses find their perfect properties. Our commitment to excellence, integrity, and customer satisfaction has earned us the trust of clients across the country.

At ATHARRYS PROPERTIES, we believe that real estate is more than just buying or selling property—it's about building dreams, creating wealth, and securing futures. Our team of experienced professionals is dedicated to making your real estate journey smooth, successful, and stress-free.

Our Mission

To provide exceptional real estate services that exceed client expectations through transparency, professionalism, and innovative solutions. We are committed to helping our clients achieve their property goals while building lasting relationships based on trust and integrity.

Our Vision

To be Nigeria's most trusted and innovative real estate company, setting the standard for excellence in property services. We aim to revolutionize the real estate industry by leveraging technology, expertise, and customer-centric approaches.

Our Values

Integrity, Excellence, Innovation, Customer Focus, and Teamwork are the core values that guide everything we do. We believe in doing business the right way, always putting our clients first, and continuously improving our services.

Our Journey

A timeline of our growth and achievements over the years

2009

Company Founded

ATHARRYS PROPERTIES LIMITED was established in Lagos with a team of 5 dedicated professionals and a vision to transform real estate services in Nigeria.

2012

Expansion to Abuja

Opened our first branch office in Abuja, expanding our reach and services to the Federal Capital Territory.

2015

Property Management Division

Launched our comprehensive property management services, helping landlords manage their properties efficiently and profitably.

2018

Commercial Real Estate Focus

Expanded our commercial real estate division, offering specialized services for office spaces, retail properties, and industrial buildings.

2020

Digital Transformation

Launched our online platform and virtual tour services, adapting to changing market needs and enhancing customer experience.

2024

National Recognition

Awarded "Best Real Estate Company in Nigeria" and expanded to Port Harcourt, Ibadan, and other major cities.
